Explain

When approaching a homeowner, our goal is to provide a straightforward and professional explanation. This ensures that the homeowner is aware of potential risks without feeling pressured or alarmed.

Guidelines for Explaining to the Homeowner

Inform the homeowner why their property might be affected by the pest issue observed on the property or nearby. Highlight the normalcy of such occurrences to alleviate concerns.

  • Identify the reason for concern: Explain that the proximity to an active pest issue could potentially impact their property.
  • Normalize the situation: Assure them that these issues are common and can be managed effectively.
  • Maintain a professional tone: Use neutral language to keep the interaction calm and informative.

Sample Script Lines

  • "It's quite common for these problems to arise, especially this time of year."
  • "I want to make sure you're informed so you can take any steps you feel necessary."

Offering this information in a succinct and reassuring manner demonstrates our commitment to ethical and considerate communication.
