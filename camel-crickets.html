General Pest Protocol
Camel Crickets
Reduce camel cricket populations through heavy glue board deployment, void and harborage treatment, exterior barrier and granular application, and correction of the damp conditions they require. Camel crickets are harmless to people but will damage stored fabric, paper and cardboard.
YEAR ROUND

1
Arrival and Intro

Park in the road if possible, or on the side of the house. Clock in to app in FieldRoutes. Knock and ring the doorbell (Mandatory).

Ask where they are seeing crickets and how long it has been an issue. Ask specifically about the crawlspace, basement, garage, and utility areas. Ask whether they have had drainage problems, standing water, or a damp crawlspace. Ask whether they have noticed damage to stored fabric, cardboard boxes or paper.

2
Identification and Biology

Camel crickets are among the most alarming-looking pests we handle and among the most harmless. Confident explanation resolves most of the customer's concern on the spot.

Appearance

1/2 to 1.5 in, tan to dark brown, often mottled or banded. Distinctly humpbacked with no wings, very long antennae and oversized hind legs. Also called cave crickets or spider crickets.

Behavior

Strictly nocturnal. Requires consistently damp harborage. Does not chirp - it has no wings to stridulate with, so a customer reporting chirping has field or house crickets instead.

They Jump Toward You, Not Away. Camel crickets have poor vision and their defensive response is to leap at whatever disturbs them. This is startling but harmless - they have no venom, do not bite meaningfully, and pose no health threat. Telling the customer this up front is often the most valuable thing you do on the visit.
They Do Cause Damage: Camel crickets feed on fabric, paper, cardboard and fungi. In heavily infested basements and crawlspaces they will chew stored clothing, boxes and paperwork. Take reported damage seriously and note it in FieldRoutes.
3
Inspection

This is a moisture inspection. Camel crickets cannot persist without damp harborage, so find the moisture and you have found the population.

Interior Priority Areas

Crawlspaces: The single most common source. Check along sill plates, around piers, in corners, on the vapor barrier, and near the access door. Look for high humidity, standing water and damp soil.

Basements: Floor-level corners, behind and under stored boxes, around floor drains, sump pits, and along the base of foundation walls.

Garages and utility areas: Along walls, behind stored items, around water heaters, and near any plumbing penetration.

Look for live crickets, shed skins, and dark specks of frass in corners and on stored items.

Exterior Harborage

Mulch beds, leaf litter, groundcover, stacked stone, landscape timbers, firewood piles and debris against the foundation.

Wells and window wells, under decks and steps, and any shaded area that stays damp.

Moisture Sources to Document

Poor crawlspace ventilation, missing or torn vapor barrier, plumbing leaks, downspouts discharging at the foundation, poor grading, and over-irrigation.

Record these carefully - they drive the recommendations and are what actually resolves the account.

4
Control - Interior and Crawlspace

Glue boards do a great deal of the work on camel crickets. They are large, ground-dwelling and travel along walls, so they capture readily.

📌 Glue Board Placement

Place glue boards generously along walls and in floor-level corners of crawlspaces, basements and garages, and near the crawlspace access door.

Use plenty of them. Glue boards deliver immediate visible population reduction and give a clear read on where activity is concentrated for the follow-up visit.

Place out of reach of pets and children.

Trapper Max Glue Boards - Bell Labs
🌪 Void and Sill Plate Dusting

Apply D-Fense Dust along crawlspace sill plates, into cracks and crevices, around piers, and into wall voids via plumbing penetrations and outlet plates in basements and garages.

D-Fense Dust (Deltamethrin) - CSI
💧 Liquid Application

With the B&G on a fine fan spray, treat along baseboards in affected areas, floor-level corners, behind and under stored items, and around plumbing penetrations.

Suspend PolyZone (Deltamethrin) - Envu
5
Control - Exterior

Exterior work reduces the population pressing in from harborage and intercepts crickets at entry points.

💧 Power-Spray Barrier

Treat the foundation 3 ft up the exterior wall and 4 ft out on the ground. Fan spray mulch beds and groundcover heavily so product reaches below the surface.

Treat around all doors, thresholds, garage door seals, windows, window wells, weep holes, utility penetrations and crawlspace vents and access doors.

Route Mix
🌾 Granular Application

Apply granule around the structure, walking 7 to 9 ft from the foundation. Focus heavily on mulch beds, groundcover and leaf litter zones where granule carries down into the harborage layer.

Wisdom EZ or Wisdom G L/N (Bifenthrin) - per seasonal rotation
🌪 Entry Point Dusting

Dust foundation cracks, weep holes, behind the siding starter strip, and around crawlspace vents without puncturing vent screens.

D-Fense Dust (Deltamethrin) - CSI
6
Customer Recommendations - Moisture and Harborage

Camel crickets are a moisture problem first and a pest problem second. Chemical treatment reduces numbers, but the population returns unless the damp harborage is corrected.

RecommendationWhy It Works
Dry out the crawlspaceThe highest-impact change available. A dehumidifier, proper ventilation, or encapsulation removes the humidity reservoir sustaining the population.
Repair or install a vapor barrierA torn or missing barrier lets ground moisture saturate the crawlspace continuously.
Fix plumbing leaksLeaks under sinks, at water heaters and in crawlspace lines create exactly the damp microclimate they need.
Correct exterior drainageExtend downspouts away from the foundation, clear gutters, and regrade low spots so water moves away from the structure.
Thin mulch and clear leaf litterReduces exterior harborage right at the entry point. Keep mulch depth to 2 inches or less and pulled back from the wall.
Move firewood and debris awayStore firewood off the ground and well away from the structure. Remove stacked stone and debris piles.
Reduce basement clutterCardboard boxes on damp floors are both harborage and a food source. Switch to sealed plastic totes off the floor.
Seal entry pointsInstall door sweeps and garage door seals, caulk foundation cracks, and repair crawlspace vent and access door screens.
Suggested Customer Talk Track

"These are camel crickets - no wings, so they don't chirp, and they can't hurt you at all. The thing that freaks people out is they jump AT you instead of away, but that's just because they can barely see. What they need is damp, and that crawlspace is holding a lot of moisture. I've put glue boards down there and treated the harborage, but the real fix is getting that space dried out - a dehumidifier or getting that vapor barrier patched would do more than anything I can spray."

7
Post Service and Follow-Up

Complete a detailed service report in FieldRoutes documenting where activity was found, moisture conditions observed, glue board locations, all treatment areas, and any damage to stored items.

Report to the customer, emphasizing the moisture connection. Get a signature on the iPad and leave a review card.

Ensure all back yard gates are secure and all equipment is loaded in your vehicle.

Setting Expectations

Advise the customer they may see a temporary increase in sightings immediately after treatment as crickets are flushed from harborage.

Follow up to check and replace glue boards, reassess activity, and retreat harborage where needed. Glue board counts are the clearest measure of whether the population is declining.

Where moisture conditions are not corrected, expect recurring pressure. Reinforce this at every visit until it is addressed.
