Residential - Quarterly Pest Control
Mosquito Service
To control and reduce current mosquito activity on the exterior of the home, and to reduce mosquito larvae breeding on site.
APR - OCT
Field Identification
Mosquito
Culicidae spp.
Size: 1/8 to 3/8 in. Key marks: Long piercing proboscis, narrow wings with scales along the veins, long slender legs. Behavior: Adults rest on the underside of foliage and in shaded harborage during the day. Breeding: Any standing water - completes the cycle in very small volumes such as a bottle cap or saucer.
Personnel Scope
Certified Pest Control Technician
Frequency
Monthly (Every 30 Days)
Materials & Equipment
Liquid Insecticides, Mosquito Blower, IGR Shaker, PPE
PPE Required
Gloves Safety Glasses Hat Pants Boots Long Sleeves
1
Arrival and Intro

Park in the road, if possible, or on the side of the house. Clock in to app in FieldRoutes. Knock and ring the doorbell (Mandatory). Inquire about their needs and anything they would like addressed.

2
Inspection

Inspect the exterior of the home for conducive conditions and breeding sites. To help reduce general pest re-services and follow ups, also inspect the exterior of the home from top to bottom for conducive conditions, foraging avenues and pest sources. Look for wasp nests, dirt dauber, spider webs and other pest debris.

Common Breeding Sites to Identify

Mosquitoes can complete their life cycle in a very small amount of standing water. Locate and note every potential breeding site during your inspection - these become your IGR treatment targets in Step 4.

Breeding Site What to Look For
Bird baths & fountains Standing water refreshed infrequently; algae or organic buildup
Tires & containers Any container holding water - buckets, toys, planters, saucers
Gutters Clogged sections holding standing water and organic debris
Tree holes Cavities and hollows in trunks and limbs that collect rainwater
Pool covers & tarps Pooled water on top of covers; sagging tarps and equipment covers
Urns, water gardens, rain barrels Decorative water features and collection barrels without circulation
Low-lying areas Depressions and drainage areas where rainfall gathers after a storm
3
Mosquito Treatment with Blower

Make sure you have your blower on setting 2.5. A standard treatment area is approximately 1/4 acre surrounding the home.

Treat all the grass, landscape areas, and trees up to 8 ft. Treat all potential resting areas such as decks, railings, swing sets, patio furniture, etc.

Apply product deep into bushes and foliage plants by using a circular/vortexing motion. This drives the material into the underside of foliage where adult mosquitoes rest during the day.

Product Temprid SC Ultra 0.05%
Active Ingredient Cyfluthrin, Cyanomethyl Opanercarboxylate
Manufacturer Envu
Mix Rate 0.54 oz / Gallon
Blower Setting 2.5
Pollinator Protection: Prevent killing bees by avoiding drift onto non-target areas and flowering plants. Do not treat blooming vegetation. Be mindful of wind conditions and adjust your approach to keep material on target.
4
IGR - Larvicide Application

To reduce the amount of hatching mosquito larvae, treat bird baths, tires, tree holes, water gardens, pool covers, urns, gutters, fountains, and rain barrels with the proper application rate per label.

It can also be used in low lying areas where rainfall will gather. The product will activate at that time.

Product Altosid Pro G
Active Ingredient Methoprene
Manufacturer Zoecon
Application Rate 1/4 tsp per 13 sq ft  |  1/4 tsp per 50 gallons
Why IGR Matters: Adult treatment alone only addresses mosquitoes currently present. Larvicide treatment breaks the life cycle at the source and is what delivers lasting reduction between monthly services. Do not skip breeding sites identified during inspection.
5
Post Service

After the services are complete, fill out a detailed service report in FieldRoutes.

Knock on the door and report to the customer emphasizing what you did and what you found, suggesting how they can help prevent future problems, get a signature on the iPad, and leave a review card.

Ensure all back yard gates are secure, and all equipment is loaded in your vehicle.

Customer Prevention Recommendations

Dump and refresh bird baths, pet bowls, and any standing water weekly. Keep gutters clear of debris. Remove or store containers, toys, and equipment that can hold rainwater. Repair leaking outdoor faucets and irrigation. Keep grass and vegetation trimmed to reduce resting harborage. Ensure pool covers and tarps are drained so water does not pool on top.

Gate Check: Confirm all back yard gates are secured before leaving. This is a common source of customer complaints and pet escapes - do not skip it.
