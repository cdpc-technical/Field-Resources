General Pest Protocol
Scorpions
Eliminate scorpion populations through liberal crack and crevice dusting of harborage voids, liquid treatment of the structure and perimeter, control of the prey insects sustaining them, and thorough exclusion. Scorpions are nocturnal and harbor in extremely tight voids - dusting is the core of this service.

1
Arrival and Intro

Park in the road if possible, or on the side of the house. Clock in to app in FieldRoutes. Knock and ring the doorbell (Mandatory).

Ask where they are finding scorpions, whether it is mostly at night, and how long it has been occurring. Ask specifically about bathrooms, tubs and sinks, closets, garages, and crawlspaces. Ask whether there are children or pets in the home, and whether anyone has been stung.

If the customer reports a sting, direct them to seek medical attention, particularly for a child, an elderly person, or anyone with a known allergy. Do not assess the sting and do not offer medical guidance. Referring them to a physician is always the correct answer.
2
Identification and Biology

Understanding scorpion behavior is what makes this service effective. They are nocturnal, they hide in extremely tight harborage, and they are hunting the other insects in and around the structure.

Appearance and Habits

Eight legs, forward pinching pedipalps, and a segmented tail carried arched over the body with a stinger at the tip. Strictly nocturnal - hides in tight harborage during daylight and hunts at night. Can survive extended periods without food or water.

Why They Are Present

Scorpions are predators that feed on roaches, crickets, spiders, and other insects. A scorpion population indicates a steady prey supply. They also seek moisture, which is why they turn up in tubs, sinks, and bathrooms.

They Fit Through Almost Nothing: Scorpions can pass through a gap as small as the width of a credit card. This is why exclusion work and crack and crevice dusting matter far more than surface spraying on these accounts.
Blacklight Inspection: Scorpions fluoresce a bright blue-green under ultraviolet light. Where a UV flashlight is available, a nighttime blacklight inspection is by far the most reliable way to locate harborage and confirm population levels. Where a night inspection is not practical, use it in crawlspaces, garages, and other dark areas during the day.
3
Inspection

Inspect for harborage, moisture, and prey. Scorpions occupy tight voids close to the ground and rarely travel far from cover.

Exterior Priority Areas

Stone and masonry: Stacked stone, block, brick piles, retaining walls, and decorative rock. These are premier scorpion harborage and should be treated as a priority.

Ground-level voids: Under decks, steps, porches, and slabs. Under and behind AC condensers. Inside irrigation and utility boxes.

Organic material: Firewood piles, lumber, landscape timbers, mulch beds, leaf litter, and wood-to-soil contact points.

Structure: Foundation cracks, expansion joints, weep holes, siding starter strip, crawlspace vents and access doors, utility and plumbing penetrations, and window wells.

Interior Priority Areas

Bathrooms: Tubs, showers, and sinks - scorpions enter seeking moisture and cannot climb back out of smooth basins. Check behind toilets and under vanities.

Garages and crawlspaces: Floor-level corners, along sill plates, behind and under stored items, and around the garage door seal.

Living space: Closets, along baseboards, under and behind furniture, and around plumbing penetrations. Check bedding and shoes in heavily infested homes.

Look for prey evidence at the same time - roaches, crickets, and spiders. Controlling the food source is part of controlling scorpions.

4
Crack and Crevice Dusting

Dust is the primary tool on a scorpion service. Because scorpions spend nearly all of their time in tight voids and only emerge at night, a surface application alone will miss the population.

🌪 Exterior Void and Harborage Dusting

Apply D-Fense Dust liberally into all identified harborage: voids in stacked stone, block, and retaining walls, under decks and steps, behind and under AC units, into utility and irrigation boxes, and into foundation cracks, expansion joints, and weep holes.

Dust behind the siding starter strip, into gaps below siding and stucco, around utility and plumbing penetrations, and at crawlspace vents and access doors without puncturing vent screens.

D-Fense Dust (Deltamethrin) - CSI
🌪 Interior and Garage Void Dusting

Dust wall voids via outlet and switch plates, plumbing penetrations under sinks and vanities, cabinet toe kicks, and along sill plates in crawlspaces and basements.

In the garage, dust floor-level corners, voids behind and under shelving, along the garage door track, and behind the water heater.

Apply a light, even coating throughout the void - heavy deposits are less effective than a thin residual layer.

D-Fense Dust (Deltamethrin) - CSI
Be Thorough and Be Liberal in Voids: On a scorpion account, time spent dusting harborage returns far more than time spent spraying open surfaces. If you are choosing where to invest effort, invest it here.
5
Liquid Treatment

Liquid application handles scorpions traveling across surfaces at night and treats the prey insects sustaining the population.

💧 Interior and Garage

With the B&G on a fine fan spray, treat along all baseboards, 6 inches up and 6 inches out. Treat floor-level corners, behind and under stored items, around plumbing penetrations, under sinks and vanities, behind toilets, and behind appliances.

In the garage, treat baseboards, behind storage, up the garage door slides, and junctions where activity is likely.

Onslaught FastCap (Multi Active) - MGK
💧 Exterior Power-Spray Barrier

Treat the foundation 3 ft up the exterior wall and 4 ft out on the ground. Treat around all doors, thresholds, garage door seals, windows, weep holes, utility penetrations, crawlspace vents, and the siding starter strip.

Treat under decks and steps, around porch skirting, into stone and landscape areas, and around outbuildings.

Suspend PolyZone (Deltamethrin) - Envuor Bifen IT (Bifenthrin) - CSI
📌 Glue Board Monitoring

Place glue boards at floor level in corners and along walls in garages, crawlspaces, basements, and bathrooms where activity was reported. Glue boards capture wandering scorpions and give a clear read on population levels and hot spots for the follow-up visit.

Place glue boards out of reach of pets and children.

Trapper Max Glue Boards - Bell Labs
Treat the Prey: If the inspection turned up roaches, crickets, or spiders, treat for those as part of the same service. Removing the food source is one of the most effective long-term levers on a scorpion population.
6
Customer Recommendations - Harborage, Exclusion and Safety

Scorpion control depends heavily on removing harborage and sealing the structure. Cover the safety guidance as well - it genuinely reduces the risk of a sting.

RecommendationWhy It Matters
Remove stone, block, and debris pilesStacked stone and masonry are the single best scorpion harborage on most properties. Removing it eliminates the source population.
Move firewood and lumber awayStore off the ground and well away from the structure. Wear gloves when handling.
Thin mulch and clear leaf litterReduces both harborage and the prey insects scorpions feed on.
Seal the structure tightlyScorpions pass through a gap the width of a credit card. Install door sweeps and garage door seals, caulk foundation cracks and expansion joints, and seal utility penetrations.
Repair screens and ventsDamaged window screens and crawlspace vent screens are direct entry routes.
Shake out shoes and clothingCheck shoes, gloves, and clothing left on the floor or in the garage before putting them on.
Pull bedding away from wallsIn active homes, keep bed skirts off the floor and beds away from walls so scorpions cannot climb up.
Reduce exterior lightingLights draw the insects scorpions hunt. Yellow bulbs or relocated fixtures reduce the prey supply near the structure.
Fix moisture problemsRepair leaks and correct drainage. Scorpions seek moisture, which is why they end up in tubs and sinks.
Suggested Customer Talk Track

"Scorpions are nocturnal and they hide in really tight spots during the day, so most of what I did today was getting dust deep into those voids - the block wall, under the deck, the cracks in the foundation. They're also in here hunting other insects, so knocking those down helps. They can squeeze through a gap about as thin as a credit card, so door sweeps and sealing up those foundation cracks make a real difference. And just shake out shoes before you put them on."

7
Post Service

Complete a detailed service report in FieldRoutes documenting where scorpions were found, harborage conditions observed, prey insects present, all areas dusted and treated, and glue board locations.

Knock on the door and report to the customer, covering the harborage and exclusion recommendations and the safety guidance. Get a signature on the iPad and leave a review card.

Ensure all back yard gates are secure and all equipment is loaded in your vehicle.

Setting Expectations and Follow-Up

Advise the customer they may see increased scorpion activity for a period after treatment as scorpions are flushed from harborage and contact the residual. Finding dead scorpions is the treatment working.

Scorpion control typically requires multiple visits. Follow up to check glue boards, reassess harborage, and retreat voids where activity persists.

Reinforce that homes with heavy stone, block, or debris harborage on the property will receive continuous pressure until those conditions are corrected.
