General Pest Protocol
Brown Recluse
To eradicate/suppress Brown Recluse infestations in customer homes.
YEAR ROUND
Field Identification
Brown Recluse
Loxosceles reclusa
Size: 1/4 to 1/2 in body. Color: Uniform tan to dark brown, no leg banding. Key marks: Dark violin shape on the cephalothorax with the neck pointing rearward; six eyes in three pairs (most spiders have eight). Signs: Irregular off-white webbing in undisturbed voids, shed skins, flat papery egg sacs.
Personnel Scope
Certified Pest Control Technician
Frequency
Initial Treatment, Quarterly Interior, Annual Power Dusting
Materials & Equipment
Liquid insecticides, PPE, sprayers, bulb duster, glue boards, power-duster, flashlight, ext cords, screwdriver, knee pads, crawl suit
PPE Required
Gloves Safety Glasses Hat Pants Boots Long Sleeves
1
Prior to Arrival

Customers should understand that they will need to vacate the home for 2 hours post service.

Upon returning to the home, visible dust accumulation will need to be cleaned up. Recommend household cleaners on hard surfaces, vacuuming on carpeting, rugs, and upholstered furniture. Machine wash bed linens and clothing if necessary.

2
Arrival and Intro

Park in the road, if possible, or on the side of the house. Clock in to app in FieldRoutes. Knock and ring the doorbell (Mandatory).

Ask them where they have been seeing the spider activity and how long it has been an issue.

3
Inspection

Brown recluse spiders prefer hiding in dark, undisturbed areas, such as behind baseboards, in wall voids, under furniture, and in storage areas.

Shine your flashlight into dark corners, cracks, crevices, and behind objects. Look for spider webs, egg sacs, molts (full body), and the spiders themselves. Carefully move and inspect boxes, clothing, and other clutter that may provide hiding spots for spiders.

Place glue boards in likely hiding places to monitor activity and help reduce numbers by trapping - basement, closets, under furniture, etc.

Document Your Findings: Record the locations where you found spiders and the level of activity. This information will be helpful in deciding which CDPC treatment to recommend - Brown Recluse Booster or Brown Recluse Max.
4
Control

Brown Recluse control is a multi-component service. Every element below is part of the treatment - do not skip steps.

Product Active Ingredient Manufacturer Application
D-Fense Dust Deltamethrin CSI Wall voids, power dusting, garage voids
Onslaught FastCap Multi Active MGK Interior baseboards, garage
Suspend Polyzone Deltamethrin Envu Baseboard rotation, exterior power spray
Wisdom (Bifen IT) Bifenthrin AMVAC Exterior power spray, granular
Glue Boards Non-chemical monitoring Catchmaster Every room, hot spot placement
1. 🌫️ Dusting

With the rubber tipped bulb duster, apply D-Fense Dust into the wall voids on each side of the housing. Do not insert the duster deeper than the length of the rubber tip. Use D-Fense NXT aerosol in place of the duster if needed.

D-Fense Dust (Deltamethrin) - CSI
2. 💧 B&G Baseboards

Treat interior baseboards with Onslaught FastCap. For ongoing maintenance we will begin rotating this product and Suspend Polyzone.

Onslaught FastCap (Multi Active) - MGK Suspend Polyzone (Deltamethrin) - Envu
3. 📌 Glue Boards

Place glue boards in every room in the home. Focus on placing them in hot spot areas for brown recluse - under furniture, in closets, behind dressers and night stands, etc.

You will want to use a lot of these traps. A 2000 sq ft home will need an average of 20 to 40 traps.

Glue Boards - Catchmaster
Pet Households: If the customer is a pet owner, make sure glue boards are placed in spots the pet cannot reach.
4. ⚡ Power Dusting - Attic

Run extension cords from an interior outlet to the attic. Run in a way that doesn't create a trip hazard on the ladder.

Put on your PPE - Goggles, Gloves, Respirator Mask.

Add one full bottle of D-Fense Dust (1 lb) into your B&G power duster. Move to where you can reach the farthest point of the attic from the attic entry. Begin dusting.

It will come out in a cloud and the idea is to push that cloud into every crack and crevice. You will hit everything from the rafters to the ceiling joists, atop the insulation, the attic platform, HVAC, etc. From the farthest point, you will be treating yourself out of the attic.

Once you have all areas treated, do not loiter. Safely come down the ladder with all your gear and close the attic so as not to allow dust to flow inside the home.

D-Fense Dust (Deltamethrin) - CSI @ 1 lb per power duster
5. ⚡ Power Dusting - Crawlspace

Run extension cords from an exterior outlet to the crawlspace.

Put on your PPE - Goggles, Gloves, Respirator Mask, Crawl Suit, Knee Pads.

Add one full bottle of D-Fense Dust (1 lb) into your B&G power duster. Move to where you can reach the farthest point of the crawlspace from the entry. Begin dusting.

You will want to hit all the cracks and crevices, floor joists, piers, sill plates, foundation, etc. Treat your way out of the crawlspace and secure the door.

D-Fense Dust (Deltamethrin) - CSI @ 1 lb per power duster
6. 🏠 Exterior and Garage

Identify any wood or leaf piles around the home and recommend immediate removal.

Power spray the exterior of the home with Suspend Polyzone or Bifen IT (Wisdom) while focusing heavily around the foundation. Treat up to 10 ft around the base of the home.

Apply Bifen granule 10-15 ft around the base of the home.

Lastly, treat the garage with Onslaught FastCap around baseboards and other areas where spiders and Brown Recluse shed skins are noticed. Be liberal with D-Fense Dust in areas of the garage where voids are observed.

Suspend Polyzone (Deltamethrin) - Envu Wisdom / Bifen IT (Bifenthrin) - AMVAC Onslaught FastCap (Multi Active) - MGK D-Fense Dust (Deltamethrin) - CSI
Power Dusting PPE is Mandatory: Never power dust an attic or crawlspace without goggles, gloves, and a respirator mask. Crawlspace work additionally requires a crawl suit and knee pads. Dust exposure in an enclosed space is a serious respiratory hazard.
5
Post Service

Store your gear in your van. Complete a detailed and meticulous invoice and notify the customer that you are finished and the home is secure.

Reinforce Before Leaving

Remind the customer of the 2 hour vacate period and the cleanup expectations - household cleaners on hard surfaces, vacuuming on carpeting, rugs, and upholstered furniture, and machine washing bed linens and clothing if necessary.

Confirm they know where glue boards were placed, particularly in pet households, and advise them not to move or discard them.
