General Pest Protocol
Clover Mites
Reduce clover mite intrusion through a thorough exterior barrier on sun-facing walls, targeted crack and crevice dusting at entry points, and habitat modification at the foundation. Clover mites are a plant-feeding nuisance pest - they do not bite or damage the structure.
SPRING & FALL PEAK
Field Identification
Clover Mite
Bryobia praetiosa
Size: Pinhead, ~1/30 in. Color: Reddish to reddish-brown. Key mark: Front pair of legs much longer than the others and extended forward - frequently mistaken for antennae. Slow moving. Signs: Mass numbers on warm sun-facing walls and windowsills; leaves a red stain when crushed.

1
Arrival and Intro

Park in the road if possible, or on the side of the house. Clock in to app in FieldRoutes. Knock and ring the doorbell (Mandatory).

Ask where they are seeing the mites, which rooms and which side of the home, and how long it has been occurring. Ask whether they have recently fertilized the lawn, and whether landscaping or new sod has been installed close to the foundation. Ask if they have been crushing them.

2
Identification and Biology

Correct identification prevents a wasted service. Clover mites are plant feeders, not a bloodfeeding or biting pest, and they do not damage the structure or its contents.

Appearance

Very small, roughly the size of a pinhead. Reddish to reddish-brown. The front pair of legs is noticeably longer than the others and extends forward, which is often mistaken for antennae. Moves slowly.

Key Behavior

Feeds on grass, clover, and ornamental plants. Enters structures in large numbers in spring and fall, typically on the warm sun-facing sides. Does not bite, does not reproduce indoors, and cannot survive long inside.

Do not crush them. Clover mites leave a red stain on walls, carpet, drapes, and countertops that is difficult to remove. Advise the customer to vacuum them up rather than wipe or crush. This is the single most important thing to tell the customer.
Conducive Condition: Heavy clover mite pressure is almost always tied to lush, well-fertilized turf growing right up against the foundation. Nitrogen-rich lawns and new sod are prime breeding grounds.
3
Inspection

Clover mite calls follow a predictable pattern. Inspect the exterior first and focus on the sun-facing sides of the structure, typically the south and west exposures.

Exterior

Inspect where turf, groundcover, ivy, or landscape beds contact the foundation. Look for grass growing directly against the wall with no bare strip.

Check foundation cracks, weep holes, siding starter strip, window and door frames, utility and plumbing penetrations, and vents. Look for mites congregating on the warm sunny wall surface.

Note any recently fertilized turf, new sod, or heavy ornamental plantings tight to the structure.

Interior

Look along windowsills, baseboards, and window and door frames on the sun-facing sides. Interior activity is nearly always concentrated on the same side of the home as the heaviest exterior pressure.

Check for red staining already present, which tells you the customer has been crushing them.

4
Exterior Treatment

Exterior control is where this service is won. The goal is a thorough barrier on the sun-facing walls and the ground immediately adjacent to them.

💧 Power-Spray Barrier

Treat the foundation 3 ft up the exterior wall and 4 ft out on the ground, concentrating heavily on the sun-facing sides where mites congregate. Treat the turf and groundcover in that band, not just the wall.

Spray around all windows, doors, siding seams, weep holes, utility penetrations, and vents. Ensure the siding starter strip is treated.

Suspend PolyZone (Deltamethrin) - Envuor Bifen IT (Bifenthrin) - CSI
🌪 Crack and Crevice Dust

Apply D-Fense Dust into foundation cracks, weep holes, behind the siding starter strip, and around utility and plumbing penetrations where mites are entering.

D-Fense Dust (Deltamethrin) - CSI
5
Interior Treatment

Interior treatment is secondary and should be light and targeted. Mites entering the home will die on their own, so the interior application is for the ones already inside and the ones actively coming through.

💧 Targeted Application

With the B&G on a fine fan spray, treat lightly along baseboards, windowsills, and window and door frames on the affected sides only. Do not broadcast the interior.

Do not apply to areas where the customer will contact the product, and keep material off soft furnishings to avoid any staining risk.

Onslaught FastCap (Multi Active) - MGK
Vacuum First: Where mites are present in numbers on the interior, vacuum them up before treating. This removes the population immediately and eliminates the staining risk from the customer sweeping or wiping them later.
6
Customer Recommendations - Habitat Modification

Chemical treatment reduces the current population, but clover mite pressure returns every spring and fall unless the habitat right against the foundation changes. This conversation is the difference between a one-time fix and a recurring callback.

RecommendationWhy It Works
Create a bare strip at the foundationAn 18 to 24 inch band of bare soil, gravel, or stone between the turf and the wall removes the food source right at the entry point. This is the single most effective long-term fix.
Pull turf and groundcover off the wallGrass, ivy, and dense groundcover touching the foundation act as a direct bridge onto the structure.
Ease up on nitrogen fertilizerHeavily fertilized, lush turf supports far larger clover mite populations. Over-fertilizing near the foundation drives the problem.
Move ornamental plantings backShrubs and flower beds tight to the wall provide both food and a protected route up the structure.
Seal entry pointsCaulk gaps around windows, doors, and utility penetrations. Repair damaged screens and weatherstripping.
Vacuum, never crushPrevents red staining on walls, carpet, and furnishings. Dispose of the vacuum contents promptly.
Suggested Customer Talk Track

"These are clover mites - they feed on grass, they don't bite, and they can't survive in your house. The big thing is don't crush them, just vacuum, because they'll leave a red stain that's tough to get out. I treated the sunny sides where they're coming in. Long term, the fix is getting a bare strip of mulch or gravel between your grass and the foundation, and easing off the fertilizer close to the house - that's what actually stops it coming back every spring."

7
Post Service

Complete a detailed service report in FieldRoutes documenting the sides of the structure treated, entry points found, and the habitat conditions observed at the foundation.

Knock on the door and report to the customer, emphasizing the do-not-crush guidance and the bare strip recommendation. Get a signature on the iPad and leave a review card.

Ensure all back yard gates are secure and all equipment is loaded in your vehicle.

Setting Expectations

Advise the customer they may continue to see some mite activity for several days as mites already inside wall voids work their way in and die off. Activity should drop off noticeably within a week.

Clover mite pressure is seasonal and peaks in spring and fall. Homes with turf tight to the foundation may need a follow-up during the peak window until the habitat is corrected.
