General Pest Protocol
Trailing House Ants
Eradicate Odorous House Ant and Argentine Ant activity using non-repellent chemistry and a rotating gel bait strategy. These are multi-queen colonies that bud when treated with repellents - no Suspend PZ or Bifen IT, no overspray on baits, and no Fipronil products indoors.

Odorous House & Argentine Ants

1
Critical Rules - Read Before Treating

Trailing ants are treated with non-repellent chemistry and baiting. Repellent products scatter the colony and make the job harder. These rules are absolute.

Never treat ants with repellent products (Suspend PZ, Bifen IT). They detect and avoid these, which splinters the colony and spreads activity to new areas.
Never get overspray on applied baits. Contaminated bait will not be accepted and the program stalls.
Never spray Fipronil products on the interior of the home (Taurus SC, Termidor). Exterior use only.
Locate the colony for best results. Following trails back to the source lets you place bait and non-repellent where it actually reaches the queens.
2
Prior to Arrival

Customers should be instructed not to use DIY products prior to or after treatment. Store-bought repellent sprays are the most common reason these programs fail.

3
Arrival and Intro

Park in the road if possible, or on the side of the house. Clock in to app in FieldRoutes. Knock and ring the doorbell (Mandatory).

Ask where they have been seeing ant activity and how long it has been an issue. Ask directly whether they have sprayed anything themselves. Explain that their cooperation is vital to the success of the program.

4
Identification

Both species are treated the same way, but confirming which you have helps set expectations on colony size and timeline.

Odorous House Ant

~1/8 in, dark brown to black, single flattened node hidden by the abdomen. Gives off a rotten coconut odor when crushed - the definitive field test. Forms large colonies with multiple queens.

Argentine Ant

~1/8 in, light to medium brown, single node. Moves in dense, persistent trails that can run long distances. Forms very large multi-queen supercolonies that may span multiple properties.

Multi-Queen Colonies: Both species have many queens and readily bud. This is why baiting and non-repellent chemistry are essential - killing foragers on contact accomplishes nothing against the colony.
5
Inspection

You may arrive when ants are not visibly active. Work the interior and exterior together.

Interior

Typically found in the kitchen, bathroom, laundry room, or any room with a water supply - they use plumbing as a guideline.

If you are not seeing any activity, spray Spectre PS in cracks, crevices, and wall voids to flush, then apply Maxforce Fleet Ant bait in suspected foraging avenues. Inspect the exterior while waiting.

Spectre PS (Chlorfenapyr) - CSIMaxforce Fleet Ant (Fipronil) - Envu
Exterior

Walk around the foundation. Search for trailing ants and potential avenues like ivy or foliage resting against the home.

Check cracks in window caulking, weep holes, damaged weatherstripping, gaps around utility and cable penetrations, mulch beds, sidewalks, driveways, and expansion joints.

6
Control - Interior

Interior control is bait-led, supported by non-repellent liquid and a flushing aerosol. Keep them separated - product on bait kills the program.

🎯 Gel Bait Rotation

CDPC uses a rotation of 4 approved gel baits to prevent resistance in the colony.

Apply in small BB-sized dots directly to trailing avenues, wall voids, counter backsplashes, cabinet hinges, window frames, edges of appliances, cracks and crevices, and plumbing penetrations.

Apply out of reach of kids and pets. Recommend removal of competing food sources.

💧 Interior Spraying

Mix Alpine WSG at the low rate (10g per gallon). It is non-repellent - ants do not detect or avoid it.

Apply a light band along baseboards where trailing, behind appliances, door and window frames, and plumbing penetrations.

Do not spray on bait applications.

Alpine WSG 0.30% (Dinotefuran) - BASF @ 10g/gal
💨 Interior Aerosol

Use Spectre PS as a flushing agent. Also effective in cabinet voids, behind switch plates, cracks and crevices, and window tracks.

Do not apply to bait applications.

Spectre PS (Chlorfenapyr) - CSI
7
Control - Exterior

Exterior work uses non-repellent liquid at the structure plus gel bait on active trails.

💧 Exterior Spraying

Use Taurus SC (non-repellent) as a spot treatment. If performing during a Quarterly, note the application area to avoid overspraying with the power-sprayer.

Apply to active foraging trails, around entry points, foundation cracks, utility penetrations, door and window frames, base of home, siding starter strip gaps, and weep holes.

Do not use more than one foot from the foundation.

Taurus SC (Fipronil) - CSI
Avoid flowering plants to protect pollinators. Do not treat blooms and be mindful of wind conditions.
🌾 Exterior Baiting

Gel baits on the exterior, preferably in areas that stay dry and out of direct sunlight.

Apply BB-sized drops along active foraging trails, cracks, crevices, voids like weep holes, and near entry points.

8
Communication and Follow-Up

Complete a detailed service report in FieldRoutes documenting trail locations, every bait placement and which product was used, and whether the customer had sprayed previously. Bait rotation only works if the history is recorded.

Advise the customer of the following:

1. Wait 30 minutes for products to dry before allowing kids and pets in the treatment area.

2. Keep the area clean and spill free - no competition with baits.

3. Ants will continue to forage and it is not uncommon to see an increase in activity - they need to pick up bait and carry it back to the colony.

4. Don't use DIY sprays - they can make treatment ineffective and push ants to other areas.

5. The office will contact them: 3 days for interior activity, 5 days for exterior.

Suggested Customer Talk Track

"These are trailing ants and they've got multiple queens, so just killing the ones you see does nothing - we have to get bait carried back to the colony. Here's what I need you to expect: for the next few days you'll probably see MORE ants, because they're recruiting each other to the bait. That's exactly what we want. The one thing that'll wreck this is store-bought spray - that repels them, splits the colony up, and we end up chasing it around your house. Keep the counters clean so the bait is the best thing on offer and let it work."
