Carpenter Ants - Interior
Eliminate interior Carpenter Ant activity using targeted crack and crevice dust applications and interior liquid treatment to address satellite colonies and foraging workers inside the structure.
YEAR ROUND
Personnel Scope
Certified Pest Control Technician
Frequency
Initial Treatment + Follow-Up as Needed
Materials
Duster, Handheld Sprayer, Drill (if void treatment needed), PPE
PPE Required
Gloves Safety Glasses Respirator Hat Pants Boots Long Sleeves
1
Arrival and Customer Interaction

Park in the road if possible or on the side of the house. Clock in to the app in FieldRoutes. Knock and ring the doorbell (Mandatory).

Ask the customer specifically where inside the home they have been seeing ants, at what time of day, and whether activity increases at night - Carpenter Ants are primarily nocturnal. Ask if they have heard rustling or crinkling sounds inside walls, especially near moisture-prone areas like bathrooms and kitchens.

2
Interior Inspection

Carpenter Ants found indoors are most commonly foraging workers from a satellite colony established inside the structure or workers from an outdoor parent colony entering to forage. Thorough inspection is critical before treatment.

High-priority areas: Under sinks and around plumbing in kitchens and bathrooms, window and door frames, areas around skylights, attic spaces, crawlspaces, and any area with a history of moisture damage or wood rot.

Frass identification: Carpenter Ant frass is coarse and fibrous - similar in texture to pencil shavings - and may contain insect parts. This distinguishes it from termite frass which is finer and pellet-shaped. Frass indicates an active gallery nearby.

Sound detection: In quiet conditions, tap on suspected wall voids. A hollow sound combined with rustling when disturbed can indicate an active Carpenter Ant gallery inside the wall.

3
Crack and Crevice Dust Application

Temprid Dust is the primary interior tool for reaching Carpenter Ants inside wall voids, structural voids, and hard-to-reach areas. Apply using a hand duster with a crack and crevice tip.

Application Areas

Apply Temprid Dust into wall voids via outlet plates, switch plates, and pipe penetrations. Treat behind and under kitchen and bathroom cabinets, inside door and window frame voids, along the sill plate in the basement or crawlspace, and any expansion joint or crack in the foundation accessible from the interior.

Void Drilling

If a gallery is suspected inside a wall void and no accessible entry point exists, a small drill hole (approximately 1/8 inch) may be made to allow dust injection. Plug the hole with a small piece of steel wool or a wall plug after application. Always communicate with the customer before drilling and document in FieldRoutes.

Application Rate

Apply a light, even coating of dust - do not over-apply. Heavy dust deposits are less effective than a thin residual layer and can alert ants to avoid the area. A light puff every 12-18 inches along a void is sufficient.

Temprid Dust (Beta-Cyfluthrin, Imidacloprid) - Envu
4
Gel Bait Placement

Apply Maxforce Fleet Ant Gel in small BB-sized placements along identified interior foraging trails and in areas where ant activity has been observed. Gel bait is a critical complement to dust and liquid treatments, providing a slow-kill food source that workers carry back to the colony - reaching ants that may not contact treated surfaces directly.

Placement Locations

Apply small placements inside cabinet hinges, along the back of cabinet shelving, inside outlet and switch plate voids (on the interior face, not inside the electrical box), under the sink, around plumbing penetrations, behind the refrigerator and dishwasher, and along any active foraging trail on baseboards or walls. Use "bait tacos" (small folded cardboard or cut glue board plastic) as a clean placement pallet on finished surfaces.

Important Notes

Do not apply gel bait on or near surfaces treated with Alpine WSG. Residual liquid on the same surface will contaminate the bait and deter feeding. Space bait placements at least 12 inches away from any sprayed surface. Do not apply to food prep surfaces, open food storage areas, or dishes.

Maxforce Fleet Ant Gel (Fipronil) - Envu
5
Interior Liquid Application

Apply Alpine WSG at the high label rate using a small handheld sprayer for interior surface treatments. Target baseboards, behind appliances, and areas where foraging trails have been observed.

Application Areas

Treat baseboards throughout affected rooms, behind and under the refrigerator and dishwasher, under the kitchen sink cabinet interior, around plumbing penetrations, and along any identified foraging trails on floors or walls. Do not apply to food prep surfaces, open food storage, or dishes.

Mix Rate

Mix Alpine WSG at the high rate (30g per gallon) for Carpenter Ant treatments. The higher rate provides the residual needed for a larger-bodied ant species.

Alpine WSG 0.30% (Dinotefuran) - BASF @ 30g/gal
6
Moisture and Conducive Condition Recommendations

Interior Carpenter Ant infestations are almost always connected to a moisture problem. Treatment will suppress activity but long-term control requires addressing the root cause. Communicate the following to the customer:

1. Fix any plumbing leaks, especially under sinks and around dishwasher connections.

2. Address any roof leaks, gutter overflow, or moisture intrusion through the foundation or crawlspace.

3. Improve ventilation in crawlspaces and attics to reduce humidity levels.

4. Replace any water-damaged or decayed wood identified during inspection.

5. Seal gaps around plumbing penetrations and utility entries where ants may be gaining access.

If active Carpenter Ant galleries are found in structural members, advise the customer to consult a licensed contractor for a structural assessment. Document all findings thoroughly in FieldRoutes and notify your manager.
7
Post-Service Communication and Follow-Up

Before leaving, advise the customer of the following:

1. Keep children and pets away from treated surfaces until dry (approximately 30-45 minutes).

2. Do not clean or wipe down baseboards and treated surfaces for at least 2 weeks to preserve the residual.

3. They may see increased ant activity for up to 7 days as the treatment takes effect.

4. If activity does not begin to decline within 2 weeks, or if new frass deposits are found, schedule a follow-up immediately.

5. The exterior protocol should be completed in conjunction with or shortly after the interior treatment for maximum effectiveness.

Best Practice: Interior and exterior Carpenter Ant protocols should be performed together whenever possible. Interior treatment alone without addressing the exterior source colony will result in reinfestation.
