General Pest Protocol
American Roaches / Palmetto Bugs
Eliminate active American cockroach activity by addressing moisture sources, treating high-harborage zones, sealing structural entry points, and applying products strategically. Unlike German roaches, these enter from crawlspaces, sewers and plumbing rather than breeding in kitchen harborage.
YEAR ROUND
Field Identification
American Roach
Periplaneta americana
Size: 1.5 to 2 in - the largest roach we encounter. Color: Reddish-brown with a pale yellow figure-8 pattern on the pronotum behind the head. Behavior: Prefers warm, damp, dark areas; strong flier at dusk. Origin: Enters from crawlspaces, sewer lines, plumbing and garages - not from kitchen cabinets like German roaches. Also called: Palmetto bug.

1
Arrival and Intro

Park in the road if possible, or on the side of the house. Clock in to app in FieldRoutes. Knock and ring the doorbell (Mandatory).

Ask where they have been seeing roaches and how long it has been an issue. Ask specifically about crawlspaces, garages, basements, and any recent plumbing or drainage problems. Reiterate that these are warrantied and they should not hesitate to call. Inquire about anything else they would like addressed.

2
Identification

American roaches are handled very differently from German roaches. Confirm which you have before planning the service - the source and the treatment are not the same.

American Roach

1.5 to 2 in - the largest roach we encounter. Reddish-brown with a pale yellow figure-8 pattern on the pronotum behind the head. Strong flier at dusk. Enters from crawlspaces, sewers, plumbing and garages.

vs German Roach

1/2 to 5/8 in, light brown with two dark parallel stripes on the pronotum. Lives indoors in kitchen and bathroom harborage and breeds there. A German roach job is an interior cleanout, not a perimeter service.

Also Called Palmetto Bugs. Customers frequently use this name. It refers to the same insect and carries no different treatment implication.
3
Inspection

American roaches are a moisture and entry-point pest. Your inspection is looking for where they are coming in and what is keeping them comfortable.

Exterior

Look for moisture-rich areas - mulch beds, irrigation points, A/C condensate drains.

Identify cracks around the foundation, utility penetrations, sewer clean-out areas, low level vents, woodpiles, and landscape timbers.

Roaches can also enter through garage doors, door thresholds, weep holes, siding gaps, and along utility and plumbing lines.

Interior

American roaches typically come from crawlspaces, sewer lines, plumbing lines, or garage entry points - not typical kitchen cabinets like German roaches.

Focus on kitchens, laundry rooms, bathrooms, utility rooms, water heater closets, and basements.

Document conducive conditions - leaks, standing water, heavy mulch, gaps in doorways, and window sills. These drive the recommendations and support the follow-up conversation.
4
Control - Exterior

The exterior is where this service is won. Treat the perimeter, the entry points, and bait the harborage.

💧 Power-Spray

Treat the foundation 3 ft up and 4 ft out.

Spray possible entry points: weep holes, water spigots, plumbing entry points, around garage doors and door thresholds, and where mulch beds meet the structure.

Route or Start Mix
🌪 Crack and Crevice Dusting

Use D-Fense Dust for cracks and crevices around the foundation and entry points.

D-Fense Dust (Deltamethrin) - CSI
🌾 Exterior Baiting

Bait exterior with Niban Granular bait in hotspots around the foundation perimeter, mulch and landscaping beds, under decks, and around A/C slabs.

Avoid heavy piles - use the shaker for light, even distribution.

Niban Granular Bait (Orthoboric Acid) - Nisus
Tip: Coat a glueboard with Niban and slide it under appliances, cabinets, and other hard-to-reach areas. Invict AB Insect Paste can also be used in homes and businesses with heavy activity.
5
Control - Interior

Interior work targets the routes roaches use to move from crawlspace, plumbing and garage into living space.

💧 Baseboards and Garage

Use Suspend PolyZone on interior baseboards and the garage.

Suspend PolyZone (Deltamethrin) - Envu
🌪 Crack and Crevice

Treat crack and crevice with D-Fense Dust or D-Fense NXT, concentrating around plumbing penetrations, under sinks, and water heater closets.

D-Fense Dust (Deltamethrin) - CSID-Fense NXT - CSI
📌 Monitoring

Glueboards can also be used to reduce numbers in high traffic areas and to show where activity is concentrated for the follow-up visit.

Glue Boards
6
Communication and Follow-Up

Complete a detailed service report in FieldRoutes documenting entry points found, moisture conditions observed, areas treated, and bait placements.

Report to the customer and reinforce the warranty. Get a signature on the iPad and leave a review card.

Ensure all back yard gates are secure and all equipment is loaded in your vehicle.

Setting Expectations

Follow up in 7 days.

Occasional sightings may occur as roaches emerge from treated voids. This is expected and is the treatment working.

RecommendationWhy It Matters
Fix drainage and moisture issuesAmerican roaches need damp harborage. Correcting leaks, condensate drainage and standing water removes what sustains them.
Seal gaps around pipes, doors and windowsClosing the plumbing, utility and threshold gaps cuts off the primary entry routes from crawlspace and exterior.
Thin heavy mulchDeep mulch against the foundation holds moisture and provides harborage right at the entry point.
Move woodpiles and timbersStored wood in contact with soil near the structure is prime harborage.
Suggested Customer Talk Track

"These are American roaches - what a lot of folks call palmetto bugs. Unlike German roaches they're not breeding in your kitchen; they're coming in from the crawlspace and the plumbing lines, so most of my work today was on the outside and at the entry points. You may still see a few over the next week as they come out of the treated voids - that's them dying, not a failure. Getting that condensate line draining away from the house would take away a big part of what's drawing them in. And these are warrantied, so just call if you keep seeing them."
